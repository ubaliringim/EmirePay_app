# Emir Pay Launchpad

Build a landing page and authentication pages (Sign Up and Log In) for a fintech company called Emir Pay. This is a digital payments platform offering airtime, data, bill payments, and financial services across Nigeria. Design it to feel premium, trustworthy, and modern — this is a money app, so it needs to inspire confidence at first glance. Use your best judgment on layout, visual style, and structure to make it stand out rather than look like a generic template.

Pages needed:

Landing page (single page with sections below)
Sign Up page
Log In page

Include working navigation between all three, plus a shared header/footer on the landing page.

Landing Page Sections

1. Navigation Bar
   Logo/wordmark "Emir Pay", nav links (Services, About, Leadership, Contact), and Log In / Sign Up buttons.

2. Hero Section
   Headline focused on the core value prop: one platform for airtime, data, bills, and payments — fast, secure, affordable. Include a subheadline expanding on this, a primary CTA ("Get Started" / "Create Account") and secondary CTA ("Log In"). This should immediately communicate trust and simplicity.

3. Services Section
   Showcase all services as individual cards/items, each with an icon and short description:

Airtime Recharge
Mobile Data Purchase
Electricity Bill Payment
Cable TV Subscription (DStv, GOtv, StarTimes)
Wallet Funding
Airtime-to-Cash Conversion
Educational PINs (WAEC, NECO, JAMB, NABTEB)
API Services for Businesses
Agent and Reseller Solutions
School Registration Services

4. Why Choose Emir Pay
   Highlight these points (icons + short text):

Fast and secure transactions
Affordable prices
Reliable service
Easy-to-use platform
24/7 availability
Excellent customer support

5. About Section
   Include:

About Emir Pay (company description: a digital payment and fintech company offering fast, secure, affordable services — airtime, data, bill payments, transfers, wallet funding — for individuals, businesses, agents, and resellers)
Our Vision: To become one of Africa's most trusted digital payment platforms by providing innovative, secure, and reliable financial solutions.
Our Mission: To simplify digital payments by providing fast, secure, affordable, and easy-to-use financial services that improve everyday life.
Our Goal: To build a trusted platform where customers manage all everyday payment and digital service needs in one place, with plans to expand services over time.

6. Who We Serve
   List of customer segments as a clean grid: Individuals, Students, Small Businesses, Corporate Organizations, Agents, Resellers, Developers (API users).

7. Leadership Section
   A "Meet Our Leadership" section with a grid of leadership team cards. Since I haven't provided actual names/photos/titles yet, use placeholder structure (avatar placeholder, name placeholder, title placeholder, short bio placeholder) for 3–4 team members so I can swap in real content later. Make this section feel executive and credible.

8. Contact Section
   Display:

Email: emirpay@gmail.com
Phone: 09060659999
Address: Hauran Wanki Sharada Masallaci street, Kano State, Nigeria
Include a simple contact form (Name, Email, Message) alongside the contact details.

9. Footer
   Logo, brief tagline, quick links (Services, About, Contact), social media icon placeholders, copyright line, and contact info repeated briefly.

Authentication Pages

Sign Up Page
Fields: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Password, Confirm Password. Include terms & conditions checkbox, primary "Create Account" button, and a link to the Log In page for existing users. Keep the form focused and uncluttered, with the Emir Pay branding present.

Log In Page
Fields: Email/Phone, Password. Include "Forgot Password" link, "Log In" button, and a link to the Sign Up page for new users. Keep it visually consistent with the Sign Up page.

Both auth pages should feel secure and reassuring given this is a financial platform — this matters more here than almost anywhere else on the site.

General Requirements
Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop
Make the leadership section easy to update later with real names, titles, and photos
Ensure smooth navigation/scroll between landing page sections
Prioritize clarity, trust, and ease of use throughout — this is a financial services product, and design decisions should reinforce credibility
NOTE: use the attached design system
